# B4 adversarial review — supplement

Everything in `pipeline/reviewer-instructions.md` applies. This sheet
adds the traps specific to the B4 families (time-lords, lunations and
eclipses, planetary returns, solar condition).

## B4-specific checks

1. **Cycle-claim accuracy.** The tempos the essays may state are pinned
   in `writer-instructions-b4.md`. Verify every stated length and
   recurrence: profection years advance per birthday; ZR chapter lengths
   must NOT be stated in years; firdaria majors' lengths only as given
   (Sun 10, Venus 8, Mercury 13, Moon 9, Saturn 11, Jupiter 12, Mars 7,
   nodes 3 and 2); no exact sub-period lengths anywhere; vimshottari
   maha lengths only if correct (Ketu 7, Venus 20, Sun 6, Moon 10,
   Mars 7, Rahu 18, Jupiter 16, Saturn 19, Mercury 17); New Moon in a
   given house about once a year; eclipse seasons about twice a year,
   the series holding an axis for a year or two; return ages only where
   the cell guarantees them (numbered Saturn returns; Uranus ~84,
   Chiron ~50, nodal ~18–19, Jupiter ~12 are cell-guaranteed by body).

2. **Selector overreach.** A year-house essay must not name a sign or a
   lord; a year-lord essay must not name a house; a ZR essay must not
   name the lot, a peak, or the chapter's length; a lunation-house essay
   must not name a sign or claim eclipse status; the eclipse on-natal
   cells must not name a specific planet; solar-phase essays must not
   assume natal-only or transit-only framing.

3. **Fatalism drift.** Eclipse and node-lord (Rahu/Ketu, nodal-return)
   prose is where doom creeps in. No disasters, no "expect loss",
   no karmic debt collection framed as fact. Same-lord verdicts
   ("Saturn antardasha in Saturn mahadasha is the hardest...") must be
   hedged as tradition's report, not certainty.

4. **Register drift.** Vedic entries must gloss Sanskrit terms on first
   use and add no terminology beyond Rahu/Ketu/mahadasha/antardasha.
   Firdaria and ZR entries stay in plain English. The technique
   explainer sentence (allowed at the top of an entry) must be one or
   two sentences, not a lecture.

5. **Shared skeletons.** The pair families (firdaria subs, dasha antars)
   are 49 and 81 sibling essays: break any repeated opening formula,
   repeated "X brings its flavor to Y's chapter" scaffolding, or
   duplicated closers across a slice or across slices.

6. **Second person and biography.** As B1/B2: hedge life events,
   degender caretakers and partners, no era claims, no age claims beyond
   the guaranteed ones, no financial or medical directives.

Fix wording in place; keep `id`/`family`/`when`/`atomIds` untouched;
re-run `check-texts.mjs` per file after edits.
