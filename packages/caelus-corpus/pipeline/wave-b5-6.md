## Wave 5 rulings, by family

**lot-sign / lot-house.** A cross-file 6-gram sweep now backs the
review. Banned framings (each already in two files): "is not a
planet but a...", "in your chart the Lot of...", "with no planet on
it...", "the distance between two places in...", "The Lot of X is
the..." as an opener shared between a lot's sign and house files.
The house-definition list is exhausted for houses 2, 3, 7, 8, 9, 11:
gloss the house as a relation to your lot's subject, never a
four-item list. The "is not X; it is Y" closer is capped at two per
slice. Mandated same-lot read: the sign/house pairs that actually
collide share a ruler or signification (9th/Sagittarius,
8th/Scorpio, 5th/Leo, 4th/Cancer, 3rd/Gemini, 6th/Virgo), so
necessity-signs reads necessity-houses hardest at Gemini/Virgo
against h3/h6, and courage-houses reads courage-signs hardest at h1
against Aries and h8 against Scorpio. Saturated beats needing new
ground: Taurus holding-past-expiry (four lots deep), Virgo
perfectionism-blocks-action (three), and the sign remedies "name the
want" (Cancer), "let one person in" (Scorpio), "say the no early"
(Libra). The remedy, not just the gloss, must be angled through the
lot's subject.

**star-contact.** The mars slice closes the family. From the venus
review: the slice-wide opener (aphoristic hook, then "Venus on X,
the [epithet]" in sentence 2) shipped 11 of 15 even after repair;
the mars slice does not inherit it: mix star-first, scene-first, and
deferred-contact openers. The remedy-label copula ("The craft to
learn is... / The repair is... / The skill this contact asks...") is
spent as a shape, all nouns included. Derive the hazard from the
star, not from the generic "gift is the fast half, discipline is the
follow-through" arc. Content independence now runs four ways: star
essay, sun, moon, and venus contacts. Mars is drive, heat, the way
you fight and exert; no "burn the surplus" beat (used up in wave 3).

**nakshatra-pada.** Your mansions are Anuradha, Jyeshtha, Mula,
Purva Ashadha (17-20). Sign-run beats now used up: the Capricorn
quarter as "patient long-ladder operator" (four for four so far),
the Aquarius quarter as "systems view costs belonging", the Taurus
quarter as clinging/defending-the-familiar. Derive from the mansion.
The counter-practice closer ban now covers the syntax, not just the
move: no closer of the form "[small act] does more for [X] than
[grander thing]", comparative or imperative. Padas 3 and 4 drift
into restating their mansion essay's closing growth beat (three of
five wave-5 restatement repairs were pada 3 or 4): read your
mansion's last three sentences and stay off them.

**varga-d9.** Venus carries the family's heaviest dignity load
(Taurus/Libra domicile, Pisces exaltation, Virgo fall). The labeled
cells must not all open on the label; the fall-arc break has now
been done two ways (mercury: mechanism-first; jupiter: gift before
label) and Venus-in-Virgo needs a third route or the break becomes
the formula. Named family-wide habits to avoid: the "the [faith/
mind/heart] that matures across your committed years" stem, the "In
marriage, this [Body]..." paragraph slot (at most a third of
entries), and the per-body scaffold "Your [regard/discipline/
generosity] is [adjective]".
