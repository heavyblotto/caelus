## Wave 3 rulings, by family

**All families.** The perception closer (what other people will come
to see or say about you) is now banned corpus-wide in B5, not just
for stars; wave 3 found it arriving in lot-sign at 5 of 12. The
transmutation closer ("X stops being A and becomes B") is banned
everywhere, in any dress. Time-marker closers ("Over the years...",
"Ten years on...") are capped at a quarter of any slice.

**lot-sign / lot-house.** New ruling: the house or sign gloss must be
angled through the lot's subject, never a stock list. Wave 3 caught
victory's h7, h8, and h12 arriving at near-verbatim copies of
necessity's glosses for the same houses; same-house cells across lot
files are siblings a reader sees together, and no lint compares them.
Say what the house is *for this lot*. The role-reversal closer (in
the end you become the one who gives/backs/lends) is capped at two
per slice. The one-line aphoristic luck-hook opener is capped at a
third of a slice. Nemesis writers: the non-fatalism rule at full
strength; a reckoning point, not a doom point; no catastrophe
inventory as the essay's spine.

**star-contact.** Wave 3's central finding: the defect is content
restatement, not shared wording. Thirteen of fifteen sun contacts
leaned on their star essay's central image or its cost/growth beat,
and no lint compares the two families, so review is the only gate.
Binding for the moon slice: read each star's essay, then pick a beat
the essay does not use; the moon's meeting with the star must stand
apart from the star's own portrait. Also binding: the body-function
gloss ("The Moon is the part of a chart that...") in at most 2 of 15,
never as a bare definition sentence; the stems "Because the star
sits under your Moon..." and "Because the Moon is instinct..." are
used up; "which is what it was for" is used up; "Your Moon stands on
X" as an opener at most twice; no hazard enumeration; constellation
identification is fine and does not count as reuse.

**parallel.** parallels-04 closes the family. New ruling: the
declination gloss must sit past the third sentence in at least half
your entries; the shipped 42 all carry it early, so the late-gloss
entries are what keep the family from reading as one shape. Same-side
sense stays mandatory in every gloss.

**nakshatra-pada.** The hinge ban is now positive, not negative: put
the navamsa sign inside a sentence that is also saying something
particular to the quarter, or name it plainly and let the next
sentence carry the content unannounced. Any sentence whose predicate
exists only to assert that the sign has an effect is the banned move,
whatever the verb. The counter-practice closer (a small remedial act
as grammatical subject) and its imperative cousin ("Sit in the
downpour first") together are capped at three per slice; alternatives:
a consequence, an observation about the mansion, a scene, a plain
statement of what stays. Deity and symbol facts are assigned, not
free: for your four mansions, pada 1 carries the deity, pada 3 the
symbol or emblem, padas 2 and 4 neither (one clause callbacks are
fine). Vary which pada number opens on the sign; wave 3 found
sign-first openings clustering on even-numbered padas.

**varga-d9.** As wave 3, plus: the "[Feeling], in you, is a form of
[noun]" apposition and the "X deserves its due, because in you it
is..." skeleton are used up (found twice across moon and mars).
