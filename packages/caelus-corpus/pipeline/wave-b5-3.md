## The general lesson wave 2 paid for

Wave 1's bans were satisfied by synonym swap. The labeled-cost pivot
came back as "The catch is / The trap is / The undertow is / The
tender spot is"; the maxim closer came back as a perception closer.
So the bans are now on the *move*, not the wording: any sentence
whose only job is to announce that the cost paragraph starts is
banned, and so is any sentence whose only job is to announce the
remedy or growth paragraph ("What ripens here is...", "The growth
this asks for is..." and every nominalized cousin). Let cost and
growth enter inside sentences that are also saying something
particular to the cell.

## New named bans and rulings, by family

**star-contact** (your family is new this wave). The 60 star essays
converged on two things you must not inherit: a fixed scaffold (lore
hook, constellation seat, "a body sits on it", attributed nature,
gift, hazard, growth) and a closer about what other people will come
to see in you. Your entries are shorter (150-400) and pointed: the
named body's function colored by the star. Do not restate the star
essay (it renders beside you), do not use the perception closer, do
not enumerate hazards ("a second hazard..."), and vary which slot the
star's character enters. The four Mars star-contact essays in wave 6
and yours now: no "burn the surplus off or it sours" beat (the Mars
parallels used it up).

**lot-sign / lot-house.** Two new bans: the phrase "this placement"
as the essay's self-reference (at most 3 per slice; use the lot's
name or restructure), and the labeled-remedy pivot above. Openings:
both wave 2 lot slices ran hook-then-explainer in every entry; give
your slice at least three architectures (open on the lot itself, open
on the house or sign matter, defer or drop the explainer). No age or
life-stage claims.

**parallel.** The family opener (aphoristic characterization of the
reader, then pair + declination gloss in sentence 2) is used up; most
of your entries must open another way (technique first, scene first,
concrete tendency first). When you gloss the declination, the
same-side sense must be in the gloss: "one declination" alone leaves
the contraparallel open. Never "equally far north or south".

**nakshatra-pada.** From the padas-01 review, binding: the tone-verb
hinge is banned outright ("[sign] tones / colors / shades / flavors
this stretch in the ninth harmonic"); the navamsa sign must land
after the opening two sentences in at least half your chips; padas of
one mansion divide the mansion's deity gloss between them rather than
each restating it; spread your lengths across the whole 120-300 band
(padas-01 landed 16 chips in a 67-word spread). Vargottama ruling
(from `rohini:2`, which stands): a vargottama pada may name the
doubled sidereal sign and gloss the term, attributed to the
tradition, but reads it as consistency or a single register, never as
"strengthening" or any strength grade.

**varga-d9.** The ripening-time closer ("In time... / Over the years...
/ What time adds...") ran 12 of 12 in the mars slice before review;
at most a quarter of your entries may close on a time marker. Gloss
ruling: `varga-frame:d9` renders beside these essays and discharges
the what-is-a-navamsa duty; keep the explainer to at most a third of
entries (sun used 12/12, mars used 3/12; mars is the model). Moon D9
dignities (domicile Cancer, exaltation Taurus, fall Scorpio) follow
the standing ruling: tradition's label, never a strength grade,
never D1.

**nakshatra-moon is complete** (27/27). Pada writers checking their
