# B6 adversarial review — supplement

Everything in `pipeline/reviewer-instructions.md` applies. This sheet
adds the traps specific to the B6 families (ten-degree faces, degree
symbols).

## B6-specific checks

1. **Doctrine accuracy.** The ordinal-degree convention: the Nth degree
   of a sign spans (N−1)°00′ to (N−1)°59′ — an essay that treats the
   15th degree as 15°00′–15°59′ is wrong. Face spans: first face holds
   the 1st–10th degree, second the 11th–20th, third the 21st–30th.
   Chaldean face rulers: the order Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus,
   Mercury, Moon anchored at Mars for the first face of Aries, cycling
   continuously (Aries: Mars, Sun, Venus; Taurus: Mercury, Moon,
   Saturn; … Pisces: Saturn, Jupiter, Mars). Face images traceable to
   the tradition (Picatrix, Agrippa) and written fresh.

2. **Originality of the symbols.** Degree symbols are original to this
   corpus. Check every image against the Sabian set and the other
   public degree books (Charubel, Sepharial, La Volasfera): a symbol
   close enough to feel borrowed fails, and so does a reading that
   paraphrases one.

3. **Selector overreach.** The selector never says which point occupies
   the degree or face. An essay that names a body ("your Sun here"),
   the Ascendant or Midheaven, a house, an aspect, or the chart's sect
   overreaches. The degree or face is the only subject; the reading
   colors whatever carries it.

4. **Fatalism drift.** The malefic-ruled faces and the traditionally
   hard degrees carry weight without doom: no catastrophe forecasting,
   no classical verdicts delivered as fact.

5. **Shared skeletons.** 360 degree symbols and 36 faces are the
   corpus's largest sibling sets. Break repeated openers, repeated
   image shapes (not every symbol is a traveler, a door, a seed), and
   repeated closing moves — within a slice and against the corpus. The
   near-duplicate and formula-cluster lints gate; the review catches
   what they miss.

6. **Second person and biography.** As every batch: hedge life events,
   degender partners and caretakers, no era claims, no age claims, no
   financial or medical directives.
