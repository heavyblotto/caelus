# Backlog — formula clusters

A rhetorical *move* a family has converged on, where no two instances are
near-duplicates: nine essays opening "What you give them is X", eleven
closing on a hedge, a whole slice of sextiles explaining that the failure
mode is disuse. The lint links sentences at a lower bar than a
near-duplicate and reports the connected component, so a finding tells
you your sentence is one of nine.

This is the one that needs the most judgement, and the one no reviewer
found reliably by eye.

## How to clear it

**Break the set up, do not reword one member of it.** Where the move is
doing real work, keep it in the entries where it bears hardest and cut it
from the rest. Where it is scaffolding, cut it everywhere.

**444 findings across the corpus.**
