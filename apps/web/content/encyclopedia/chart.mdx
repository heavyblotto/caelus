# Chart

## The finished wheel

A chart is a diagram of the sky at one instant, from one place, flattened
onto the ecliptic and turned so the eastern horizon sits at the left edge.
The figure below rests at the finished wheel; scrubbing it left unfolds the
wheel back into the sky it came from.
