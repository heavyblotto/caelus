# Retrograde

## The loop in the ephemeris

A planet usually moves forward along the zodiac, but in certain
seasons it slows, stops, and backs through the degrees it just
crossed, then stops and moves forward again. The two stops are the
stations, and the backward span between them is the retrograde loop.
The figure draws the longitude line through one loop; the inset
shows the cause: the Earth overtakes the planet on the inside
track, and the line of sight to the planet swings backward against
the stars.
