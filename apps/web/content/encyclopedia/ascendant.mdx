# Ascendant

## An intersection, not an object

The horizon and the ecliptic are two great circles on the same sphere, so
they cross at exactly two points. The eastern crossing is the Ascendant.
The figure holds the derivation at its HORIZON station, where the two
circles meet; the Sun is followed through the morph.
