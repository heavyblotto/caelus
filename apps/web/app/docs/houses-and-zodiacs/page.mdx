# Houses & Zodiacs

## House systems

Caelus includes twelve built-in house systems: `placidus`, `whole_sign`,
`equal`, `porphyry`, `koch`, `regiomontanus`, `campanus`, `alcabitius`,
`morinus`, `meridian`, `polich_page`, and `vehlow`. Specify a house system by
passing its ID as the final argument to `engine.chart()`, or under the
`houseSystem` key in the options object.

<Note>
  Placidus and Koch are mathematically undefined inside the polar circles. At
  polar latitudes where quadrant cusps break down, Caelus falls back to
  whole-sign houses automatically and records both values:{" "}
  <code>chart.houseSystem</code> becomes <code>&quot;whole_sign&quot;</code>{" "}
  while <code>chart.houseSystemRequested</code> keeps your requested system.
</Note>

## Tropical and sidereal zodiacs

Charts default to the tropical zodiac. To compute sidereal longitudes, pass a
named sidereal ayanamsa via `zodiac`:

<CodeBlock
  lang="typescript"
  label="zodiac.ts"
  code={`engine.chart(1990, 6, 10, 14, 30, 0, 27.95, -82.46, {
  zodiac: "sidereal:lahiri",
});`}
/>

Five analytical ayanamsas ship out of the box: `lahiri`, `fagan_bradley`,
`krishnamurti`, `raman`, and `yukteshwar`. Two star-anchored modes
(`galcent_0sag` and `true_citra`) rely on the fixed-star catalog already
bundled in the embedded dataset; see{" "}
<a href="/docs/data-tiers">Data Tiers</a> for details.

## Angles

Every calculated chart includes `chart.angles.asc` (Ascendant) and
`chart.angles.mc` (Midheaven), alongside the vertex and east point. All
house cusps are computed using closed-form spherical trigonometry rather than
iterative approximation.
