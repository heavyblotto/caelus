## Day charts and night charts

Sect divides charts into day and night: the Sun above the horizon or
below it. The Lots of Fortune and Spirit follow sect. By day,
Fortune is the Ascendant plus the Moon minus the Sun, and Spirit
reverses the formula; by night, the two formulas swap. The figure
draws both lots on the wheel; the toggle flips the chart's sect and
the lots move.
