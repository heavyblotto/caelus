## The angle between two bodies

An aspect is an angle between two bodies measured along the ecliptic:
conjunction, sextile, square, trine, opposition. The dial draws the
aspect points from the first body's place and shades each orb zone;
the arc from the nearest point to the second body is the orb. Drag
the second body to re-read the angle, and move the orb rail to add
and remove rows in the aspectarian.
