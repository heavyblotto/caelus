## Two zodiacs, one sky

The tropical zodiac is anchored to the equinoxes, the sidereal zodiac
to the stars. Precession carries the first against the second by
about fifty arcseconds a year, so the two frameworks drift apart, and
the gap between them is the ayanamsa. The figure overlays the two
rings: the wedge between the zero marks is the gap, and the rail
walks three centuries of it.
